# Pitch Deck Structure (Claude generates fresh HTML for each client)

Generate a single-file HTML pitch deck using Tailwind CSS CDN.
Slide-style layout (full-viewport sections, scroll or click-nav).
Professional indigo/slate color scheme unless the client industry suggests otherwise.

## Required sections (in order)

### 1. Cover
- Big headline: "[COMPANY_NAME] — Your Digital Upgrade"
- Sub: "A proposal from [YOUR_COMPANY]"
- Date + your contact info

### 2. The Opportunity
- "87% of consumers research businesses online before visiting"
- "You're missing [X] potential customers every month who search Google but can't find you"
- Localize with the city/neighborhood name

### 3. What We Build For You
Visual checklist with icons:
- [ ] Modern website (mobile-first, loads in < 2s)
- [ ] AI chat assistant (answers FAQs 24/7, captures leads)
- [ ] Google-ready SEO (show up in local searches)
- [ ] Contact form + booking link (if applicable to industry)
- [ ] Social media integration
- [ ] SSL security + reliable hosting

### 4. Industry-Specific Preview
Show a wireframe or describe the key sections of the site:
- Restaurant: hero photo, menu, hours, "Reserve a Table" button, reviews
- Auto shop: services list, "Get a Quote" form, photo gallery, map
- Real estate: featured listings, agent bio, "Schedule a Showing" form
- Salon: services + pricing, photo gallery, "Book Now" button
- Generic: hero, about, services/menu, contact, location

### 5. Timeline
Week 1: Discovery call + gather your photos/content
Week 1-2: Build + internal review
Week 2: Launch + Google submission

### 6. Investment
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Website design & development | $500 (one-time) |
| Monthly maintenance & hosting | $50/month |
| *Optional: SEO deep-dive* | *+$150* |
| *Optional: Social media management* | *+$100/month* |

"No contract. Cancel anytime."

### 7. Why Us (keep brief)
- Local business — we're right here in [CITY]
- You own everything (code, domain, content)
- We specialize in [INDUSTRY] businesses
- Fast turnaround — live in 2 weeks

### 8. Next Steps
Clear CTA: "Let's hop on a 15-minute call → [PHONE] or [EMAIL]"
Or: "Reply to this email and I'll send over a mockup of your site"

## Design notes
- Use real emoji as icons (no icon library needed)
- Add subtle gradient backgrounds per section
- Mobile-responsive (client may view on phone)
- Include a "Print / Save as PDF" button at the bottom
- Keep total file size under 500KB (no external images, use CSS gradients)
