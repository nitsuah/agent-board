# Service Packages Reference

Use this when generating pitch decks, emails, and invoices.

## Starter Package — $500 one-time + $50/month
- 1-5 page static website (custom design)
- Mobile-responsive + fast (< 2s load)
- AI chat widget (Tidio free tier)
- Contact/lead-capture form (Netlify Forms)
- Basic on-page SEO (meta tags, Google sitemap)
- Netlify hosting (custom subdomain or client's own domain)
- 1 round of revisions
- Monthly: content updates (up to 2/month), hosting, monitoring

## Growth Package — $900 one-time + $100/month
Everything in Starter, plus:
- Industry-specific booking/ordering integration
- Google Business Profile optimization
- 3 blog/news posts/month (Claude-drafted, client approves)
- Monthly analytics report (Google Analytics)
- 3 rounds of revisions

## Premium Package — $1,500 one-time + $200/month
Everything in Growth, plus:
- Custom domain purchase + setup
- Google Ads starter campaign ($200 ad budget not included)
- Social media profile setup (Facebook + Instagram)
- 2 promo videos (via content-gen / MoneyPrinterTurbo — repurpose for social)
- Priority support (24h response)
- Quarterly strategy review call

## Default pitch: lead with Starter, mention Growth as upgrade path
"Most clients start with Starter and upgrade to Growth within 3 months once they see the leads coming in."

## Cold outreach positioning
- Lead with the problem: "most people search online before visiting"
- Anchor to value: "$500 is less than one month of lost business"
- Reduce friction: "2-week turnaround, you own everything, cancel anytime"
- Social proof: use the number of local businesses already served (update as you grow)
